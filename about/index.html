Between the pull of the horizon and the warmth of home.

The project

A shared musical world

Driftward is a cinematic Nordic folk music project shaped by seafaring stories, northern landscapes and the moments that bring people together.

Its songs move between distant horizons and the warmth of returning home — from ships leaving the harbor to voices rising around a longhouse fire.

Each release is another chapter in the same musical world: adventure, friendship, celebration, longing, sacrifice, hope and the freedom of the open sea.

Production & transparency

Human direction, AI-assisted creation

Driftward is created and curated by Leon Oßendorf. Music and visual material are produced wholly or partly with artificial-intelligence tools.

Suno supports the music production. ChatGPT and OpenAI ImageGen support the creation of cover artwork, hero imagery, the logo and the emblem. The necessary publication and usage rights for the music and visual material used on this website are held by the project.
